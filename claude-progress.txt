- **CRITICAL FIX**: Fixed same cards appearing every day for 5+ days
  - Root cause: Learning phase cards (step 0-2) had next_review_at = now,
    and getCardQueue() selected ALL due cards without limiting them.
    This caused same 20 cards to appear every day until graduation (4 days).

    - 14 slots for learning cards (they MUST graduate, need 3 reviews each)
    - 4 slots for graduated reviews (MIN_GRADUATED_REVIEWS)
    - 2 slots for new cards (MAX_NEW_CARDS_PER_SESSION)

- After fix: Natural progression 90% → 80% → 70% → 60% as cards graduate
